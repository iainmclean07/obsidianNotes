zibrekEffectRealisticAppearance2018 investigated how photorealism effects behavioural responses through questionnaries. Participants moved through the space using an xbox controller rather than an HMD. Found that with static agents, people increased proximity to less realisitic characters, and hypothesise that it is due to wanting to inspect the finer character details.
